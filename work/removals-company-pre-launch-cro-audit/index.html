Context

A removals company with a new site built and sitting on a staging address, not yet pointed at the live domain. Modern design, the right promises on the page - fully insured, best price guarantee, flexible booking - and a clear, benefit-led headline. They wanted one thing from the site: the phone to ring.

The problem

There was nothing to call. No phone number in the header, the hero, the call-to-action bands or the footer, and no click-to-call anywhere on mobile. Every route on the site led to a form, and the main quote form asked a household mover for a price in dollars, a freight type and a weight in kilograms - template fields nobody had replaced. All six services linked to the same Contact page, so no service had a page of its own, none could rank and none could make a specific offer. The trust signals had the same problem: two different sets of reviews with no source behind either, a claim of 1,250 trained people, and the theme's own Style Guide and Changelog pages still sitting in the footer.

Approach

The whole point of this one was its timing. Because the site was still on a staging address, none of it had cost an enquiry yet - which makes it the cheapest possible moment to fix, and the report says so in the summary rather than burying it. The order was: put a callable number in the header, the hero and every call-to-action band, and make it click-to-call on mobile; give each of the six services its own page with its own offer; strip the template fields out of the quote form and cut it down to what a mover actually needs to answer; and make every trust claim either verifiable or gone.

My role

The audit and the pre-launch plan were mine. This was a diagnosis, not a retainer, so the figures below are what the audit found before launch rather than results after a fix. The report credited what was already strong, because most of this site was: the hero headline is clear and benefit-led, the subhead names the two biggest objections in removals - insurance and price - in its first line, the four-step process and the cover FAQ do real work, and quote calls to action appear in every section, so a visitor is never far from acting. They just could not phone.
