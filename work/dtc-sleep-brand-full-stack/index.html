Context

A direct-to-consumer sleep brand selling pillows into Canada and the US. Small catalogue, high consideration, a buyer who compares three or four brands before ordering — so the job was less about reach than about being the one that gets compared on its own terms, in both the shopping results and the organic ones.

The problem

The account was spending into a narrow set of campaigns without the structured data that makes a product listing worth clicking, and organic was sitting at an average position of 10.3 — the second page, where a comparison shopper never arrives. Return on spend could not be judged account-wide because conversion value was not being read back against cost in one place.

Approach

Product, review and breadcrumb schema across the catalogue so the listings carried price, rating and structure into the results page; Core Web Vitals and crawl stats cleaned up; and the Google Ads account restructured so conversion value and cost sat in the same view and budget could follow return instead of volume. Organic and paid were then reported against each other every month, not separately.

My role

Both tracks were mine on this account: the schema work and the technical SEO fixes, the Google Ads restructure and budget decisions, and the reporting.
